# Singapore law and official guidance on nurse rest and shift work

Scope A of the nurse-rest research. Researched 2026-09-29. All quotes were fetched from the URL shown.

## Bottom line

- Singapore law sets a weekly rest day, a 12-hour daily cap, averaged weekly hours and a 72-hour monthly overtime cap. These limits sit in Part 4 of the Employment Act. Part 4 does not cover most registered nurses because of the salary threshold.
- For most public hospital nurses the binding source is the HSEU collective agreement (CA). It sets 40 hours a week for rotating shifts and 1 rest day a week.
- No Singapore statute, MOH rule, SNB rule or CA clause found here requires a day off after night shifts. It also sets no minimum hours between shifts and no cap on consecutive nights. MOH said so in Parliament.
- The Workplace Safety and Health (WSH) Act and WSH Council fatigue guidelines fill the gap. They name quick changeovers, long night runs and sleep debt as fatigue hazards.

## 1. Employment Act Part 4: who it covers

Section 35 sets the salary thresholds.
- Workmen: "to workmen who are in receipt of a salary not exceeding $4,500 a month". ([SSO, EA s.35](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/EmA1968?ProvIds=pr35-,pr36-,pr40-))
- Others: "to every employee (other than a workman or a person employed in a managerial or an executive position) who receives a salary not exceeding $2,600 a month". (same source)
- MOM confirms the same figures: "A workman (doing manual labour) earning a monthly basic salary of $4,500 or less." ([MOM, who is covered](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/employment-act/who-is-covered))
- MOM: "Part 4 of the Employment Act does not cover managers and executives." ([MOM, hours of work](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days))

What this means for nurses:
- MOH gives the median nurse base pay as "about $4,300 to $4,700" for 2021 to 2024. The quote reads "and that for nurses increased from about $4,300 to $4,700." ([MOH, 25 Sep 2025](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/average-and-median-salaries-earned-by-public-sector-healthcare-workers-from-2021-to-2025/))
- Nurses are not manual workmen, so the $2,600 threshold applies. A typical registered nurse earns above it and falls outside Part 4.
- Some junior or enrolled nurses earn less. MOH's community care example says an Enrolled Nurse "can expect to receive a starting monthly base salary of $2,050". Source: MOH community care salary guideline, seen only in a search snippet. UNCONFIRMED. I did not open the page.
- The NHG CA expects staff outside Part 4. It caps the overtime pay base for a nurse who "is not covered by Part IV of the Employment Act". ([NHG CA 2010, cl.11(3)](https://alep-p-001.sitecorecontenthub.cloud/api/public/content/e8539a55ffff44fd902b8cdf465434b5?v=5a71bd1a))

## 2. Employment Act Part 4: the limits (for staff it covers)

Rest day, s.36 ([SSO](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/EmA1968?ProvIds=pr35-,pr36-,pr40-)):
- "Every employee must be allowed in each week a rest day without pay of one whole day".
- "The employer may substitute any continuous period of 30 hours as a rest day for an employee engaged in shift work."
- "the employer must prepare or cause to be prepared a roster before the commencement of the month in which the rest days fall".
- MOM adds: "The maximum interval allowed between 2 rest days is 12 days." ([MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days))

Hours of work, s.38 ([SSO, EA s.38](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/EmA1968?ProvIds=pr38-)):
- Normal cap: "more than 8 hours in one day or more than 44 hours in one week".
- Overtime cap: "An employee must not be permitted to work overtime for more than 72 hours a month."
- Daily cap: "an employee must not under any circumstances work for more than 12 hours in any one day." This cap has exceptions, including "work, the performance of which is essential to the life of the community".
- MOM: "Work on rest day or public holidays is not counted in the 72-hour overtime limit". ([MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days))

Shift workers, s.40 ([SSO](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/EmA1968?ProvIds=pr35-,pr36-,pr40-)):
- Shift workers can pass 8 hours a day and 44 a week. The limit is: "but the average number of hours worked over any continuous period of 3 weeks must not exceed 44 hours per week."
- Same 12-hour cap: "must not under any circumstances work for more than 12 hours in any one day."

The Act says nothing about night work, rest between shifts, or rest after nights.

## 3. Collective agreements (HSEU with the clusters)

MOH on who sets the terms: terms for unionised staff are "set out in Collective Agreements negotiated between each of the public healthcare clusters and the Healthcare Services Employees' Union". Covered staff "have a stipulated maximum of 38 to 42 hours per week". ([MOH, Jan 2022](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/setting-limits-for-healthcare-workers-such-as-maximum-number-of-hours-worked-and-call-backs-allowed-and-mandating-guaranteed-time-off/))

SingHealth Staff Agreement of 2022, CA No.281/2022 ([PDF](https://alep-p-001.sitecorecontenthub.cloud/api/public/content/d08b4e8f42cb4ea9901671c7c1a7040b?v=469db8a3)):
- Scope excludes "Nursing job grades N10 and above". Ward nurses below N10 are covered.
- Cl.11(1): "Permanent night : 38 hours per week" and "Rotating shift : 40 hours per week, averaged over a 2 or 3-week roster".
- Cl.11(4): "Rotating shift staff may be rostered for morning, afternoon and night shift, or any combination thereof".
- Cl.13(1): "Every staff shall be allowed 1 rest day without pay per week."
- Cl.13(2): the rest day "shall be determined by the duty roster drawn up in accordance with the operational needs of the Institution."
- Cl.22 pays a per-shift "Night Shift Premium". Nursing N02A to N04 get "$47" weekday and "$80" weekend per night shift.
- The CA treats hours above 40 as paid overtime, not as a ban.
- The CA has no clause on rest between shifts, consecutive nights, or days off after nights.

NHG Staff Agreement of 2010 ([PDF](https://alep-p-001.sitecorecontenthub.cloud/api/public/content/e8539a55ffff44fd902b8cdf465434b5?v=5a71bd1a)) has the same hours and "1 rest day which is without pay per week".

UNCONFIRMED: newer CAs for SingHealth, NHG and NUHS after 2022. I found the HSEU list page ([hseu.org.sg](https://www.hseu.org.sg/resources/collective-agreements)) in search but did not open it. I found no NUHS CA text.

## 4. MOH position in Parliament

MOH has said plainly that it sets no rest-between-shifts minimum.
- "While the Ministry does not impose a minimum number of rest hours, duty rosters for nurses are planned in advance to ensure staff have adequate rest between shifts." (Ms Rahayu Mahzam, Senior Parliamentary Secretary, Aug 2022, [Telescope transcript](https://telescope.gov.sg/transcript/4430), also [MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/ensuring-adequate-rest-for-nurses/))
- MP Louis Ng raised quick returns: nurses "sometimes end their shift at 9.00 pm or 10.00 pm, and then they are back at work at 7.00 am, and that is insufficient rest for many of them." (same transcript)
- Reply: "Duty rosters are planned in advance and there are guidelines, so that they are not working consecutively beyond a certain number of days". The number was not given. UNCONFIRMED what the cluster limit is.
- MOH does "not impose a hard cap on the number of working hours and number of call backs." ([MOH, Jan 2022](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/setting-limits-for-healthcare-workers-such-as-maximum-number-of-hours-worked-and-call-backs-allowed-and-mandating-guaranteed-time-off/))
- Junior doctors have a formal cap ("should not exceed 80 hours" a week averaged over a month). Nurses have no equivalent. (same source)

Recent reforms are about flexibility, not rest rules.
- "the nurses can choose from nine shifts, instead of being assigned to only three regular shifts" at TTSH. "Singapore General Hospital, for instance, has 78 flexible shift combinations." ([SGH/Straits Times, 18 May 2024](https://www.sgh.com.sg/news/careers/hospitals-in-singapore-make-flexible-shifts-more-accessible-to-nurses))
- Minister Ong, 7 Jul 2025: "Hospitals like Tan Tock Seng Hospital offered more flexibility in working hours, and shortened handover time significantly." ([MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/speech-by-mr-ong-ye-kung--minister-for-health-and-coordinating-minister-for-social-policies--at-the-2025-nurses--merit-award/))
- UNCONFIRMED: any MOH move to 12-hour nursing shifts, or any post-2022 PQ on nurse rest. I searched moh.gov.sg and general news and found none.

## 5. WSH Act and WSH Council fatigue guidance

The WSH Act gives the general legal duty. It covers fatigue risk without naming it.
- s.12(1): "It is the duty of every employer to take, so far as is reasonably practicable, such measures as are necessary to ensure the safety and health of the employer's employees at work." ([SSO, WSHA s.12](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/WSHA2006?ProvIds=pr12-))
- s.12(2) extends the duty to "persons (not being the employer's employees) who may be affected", which covers patients.

WSH Guidelines on Fatigue Management ([PDF](https://www.tal.sg/wshc/-/media/tal/wshc/programmes/files/fatigue-management.ashx), [topic page](https://www.tal.sg/wshc/topics/fatigue/fatigue-management)):
- "Currently, there are no national standards on fatigue management."
- Hazards: "Insufficient recovery time between shifts" and "Timing of shifts (e.g. night shift)".
- Sleep debt: "A continual sleep of less than that required will incur a sleep debt that cannot be recovered within one day. Recovery usually requires a few days of adequate sleep."
- "Limit shift work to not more than 12 hours including overtime".
- "Avoid quick shift changeovers, such as finishing at 11pm and starting again at 7 am".
- "Adopt forward-rotation shift (i.e. morning to afternoon, afternoon to night), if shift rotation cannot be avoided".
- "Keep or limit night shift work to a minimum."
- "Plan shift schedules ahead of time and communicate them to employees".
- Case study: "12-hour night shifts should be limited to two to three consecutive nights, as advised by established guidelines." The case was a driver who had worked "four consecutive night shifts".

MOM guidance:
- MOM PQ reply on shift work sleep disorder, 8 Apr 2025: employers reduce risk "such as scheduling shifts to allow for sufficient rest between shifts." ([MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/parliament-questions-and-replies/2025/0408-written-answer-to-pq-on-shift-work-sleep-disorder))
- Tripartite Guide on Fitness for Work (oil and chemical shift staff, cited by MOM as usable elsewhere): "Shift schedules should allow for sufficient rest between shifts and working hours shall adhere to the provisions and conditions under the Employment Act." ([MOM PDF](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/safety-health/tripartite-guide-fitness-for-work.pdf))
- Tripartite Advisory on Mental Health and Well-being (2023 ed.): "If employees are required to work after-hours, supervisors should consider giving time-off for employees to have adequate rest". ([TAL PDF](https://www.tal.sg/tafep/-/media/tal/tafep/resources/advisories/files/tripartite-advisory-on-mental-health-and-well-being-at-workplaces.pdf))

## 6. SNB and SNA

Principle 4: they must "report unsafe clinical practices that could potentially compromise People's safety". ([SNB Code PDF](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/78/5cf13dde-b80c-448c-b3e9-f18f267408aa/code-for-nurses-and-midwives-april-2018.pdf))

## Implications for the roster rules

Legal status in one line: Singapore law is silent on rest after nights, rest between shifts and consecutive nights. The WSH Act duty plus WSH Council guidance fill that gap. The user's call to treat rest after nights as safety is consistent with that duty. It is a policy choice, not a statutory rule.

Coverage choice: Part 4 limits bind only staff at or under $2,600 basic. I propose applying them to every nurse as a floor. One rule set is simpler than per-salary rules, and no source forbids it.

| # | Rule | Label | Evidence | Rests on |
|---|------|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | At least 1 rest day per 7-day week. For a shift worker a rest day is a whole calendar day, or 30 continuous hours off. | HARD | strong | EA s.36(1)-(2), SingHealth CA cl.13(1) |
| 2 | No more than 12 days between two rest days. | HARD | strong (Part 4 staff), conservative default for others | MOM hours page |
| 3 | No more than 12 hours of work in one day, overtime included. | HARD | strong | EA s.38(8), s.40(3), WSH guideline |
| 4 | Overtime at most 72 hours per month. | HARD | strong (Part 4 staff), conservative default for others | EA s.38(5) |
| 5 | Contracted hours average 40 per week over the roster cycle (38 for permanent night staff). Hours above count as overtime. | STRONG (contract target, overtime allowed) | strong | SingHealth CA cl.11(1), NHG CA cl.10(1) |
| 6 | Shift-worker averaging: at most 44 hours per week averaged over any 3 weeks. | HARD for Part 4 staff, STRONG for others | strong | EA s.40(1) |
| 7 | No quick return. Ban PM followed by AM next day. | HARD (safety) | moderate | WSH guideline "finishing at 11pm and starting again at 7 am", MOM 2025 PQ, 2022 PQ complaint |
| 8 | No day shift (AM or PM) on the day a night shift ends. | HARD (safety) | moderate | Follows from rule 3 and rule 7, WSH "insufficient recovery time" |
| 9 | Day off after a night block. The calendar day after the last night is not a work day. | HARD (safety policy, not law) | weak in SG law, moderate in SG guidance | WSH sleep-debt text, WSHA s.12 duty. Law is silent. Scope B international evidence fills the gap. |
| 10 | Two days off after a block of 2 or more nights. | STRONG | weak in SG sources | WSH "Recovery usually requires a few days". Relies on Scope B. |
| 11 | Maximum consecutive nights: 3 for 12-hour nights. 4 for 8 to 10-hour nights. | STRONG | moderate for 12-hour nights, weak for shorter nights | WSH case study "two to three consecutive nights" |
| 12 | Forward rotation only (AM then PM then N). No backward steps within a working run. | STRONG | moderate | WSH guideline |
| 13 | At most 6 consecutive working days. | STRONG | weak (number not published) | CA 1 rest day per week, PQ reply on unnamed cluster limits |
| 14 | Publish the roster before the month starts. | HARD (process, outside the solver) | strong | EA s.36(4), WSH "Plan shift schedules ahead" |

Solver note on rules 1 and 9: a post-night day off does not always count as the weekly rest day. A night ending 07:00 followed by an AM start 24 hours later gives only 24 hours off. That misses the 30-hour shift-worker rest day in s.36(2). The solver needs to count the post-night sleep day and the weekly rest day separately, or require 30 continuous hours.
