# Singapore ward rostering practice (Scope C)

Date: 2026-09-29. Web research only. Every claim has a URL and a short quote I saw on that page.
Forum posts are anonymous. I treat them as weak evidence of practice, not as policy.

## 1. Shift patterns and times

**Three shifts are the norm. Day shifts are about 8 hours. Nights are longer (10 to 12 hours).**

- TTSH publishes its routine ward shifts (2024-05-09). AM is "7 am to 3 pm". PM is "1 pm to 9 pm". Night is "8 pm to 8 am" and lasts "12 hours".
  https://www.ttsh.com.sg/About-TTSH/TTSH-News/Pages/Tan-Tock-Seng-Hospital-Strives-To-Make-Work-Life-Balance-A-Reality-For-Nurses-With-Unique-Flexible-Shift-Scheduling.aspx
- NTFGH (NUHS) night nurse clinician, 16 Nov 2023: her schedule "runs from 9pm to 7.30am". "9pm: I attend the handover briefing".
  https://nuhsplus.edu.sg/article/from-dusk-till-dawn--the-life-of-a-night-shift-nurse
- Straits Times (reposted by SGH, 2024-05-18): "Traditionally, nurses have three regular shifts a day". SGH "has 78 flexible shift combinations". NUHS "has more than 50 shift arrangements".
  https://www.sgh.com.sg/news/careers/hospitals-in-singapore-make-flexible-shifts-more-accessible-to-nurses
- HardwareZone thread (2021), post #122: "7am till 3.30pm. 1 pm to 10pm. 9pm to 7am." Post #85: "The pm shift usually starts at 1pm. The rest correct with +/-1 hr difference in some hospitals". Post #71: night shift "starts at 8.30pm to 7.30am next day".
  https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/some-nurse-shifts-are-like-finish-work-at-930-pm-and-then-start-work-at-7-am-the-next-day.6477324/page-7
  https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/some-nurse-shifts-are-like-finish-work-at-930-pm-and-then-start-work-at-7-am-the-next-day.6477324/page-5
  https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/some-nurse-shifts-are-like-finish-work-at-930-pm-and-then-start-work-at-7-am-the-next-day.6477324/page-4
- 12-hour day and night rotas exist mainly in crisis or special units. Mothership (Mar 2020) on SGH emergency staff: "12-hour shifts, 10 days in a row" (headline, Covid period).
  https://mothership.sg/2020/03/sgh-frontline-workers-interview/

Takeaway: the AM and PM shifts overlap in the afternoon. The night shift overlaps both ends for handover. The night is 10.5 to 12 hours.

## 2. Weekly hours and rest days (contract and law)

- HSEU collective agreements (as reported by Mothership, 2020-05): "the maximum working hours per week for nurses is 42 hours for regular shift, 40 hours for a rotating shift". Permanent night is "38 or 40 hours (depending on hospitals)". Rotating hours at NUHS are "40 hours per week (averaged over a two- or three-week cycle)". Also: "Nurses are entitled to one rest day per week".
  https://mothership.sg/2020/05/nurses-pay-singapore/
- MOH parliamentary reply (10 Jan 2022): unionised staff have "a stipulated maximum of 38 to 42 hours per week, depending on whether the worker is engaged in shift work". They are "entitled to a given number of rest days within their work-rest cycle". MOH "does not impose a hard cap on the number of working hours".
  https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/setting-limits-for-healthcare-workers-such-as-maximum-number-of-hours-worked-and-call-backs-allowed-and-mandating-guaranteed-time-off/
- MOH reply (2 Aug 2022): "While the Ministry does not impose a minimum number of rest hours, duty rosters for nurses are planned in advance". The rest day and hour limits "are stipulated in the Employment Act".
  https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/ensuring-adequate-rest-for-nurses/
- MOM, Employment Act Part 4. Rest day: "A rest day comprises 1 whole day (midnight to midnight)."
  Shift workers: "the rest day can be a continuous period of 30 hours."
  Gap: "The maximum interval allowed between 2 rest days is 12 days."
  Daily cap: "you are not allowed to work more than 12 hours a day."
  https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/hours-of-work-overtime-and-rest-days
- Part 4 covers only lower earners. For a non-workman, the limit is a basic salary of "$2,600 or less". Also "Part 4 of the Act **does not** cover all managers or executives."
  https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/employment-act/who-is-covered
  Note: most registered nurses likely earn above S$2,600 basic, so for them the collective agreement or contract is the binding rule, not Part 4. Unconfirmed: I did not find a source that states nurse Part 4 coverage directly.

## 3. Off days per roster period

- A restructured hospital nurse (HWZ post #130, 2021): "we get a fixed amount of off days per roster, restructured is 8 off days confirmed every 4 weeks". Same post: "Sometimes can be straight 8-10 days work within a two week range."
- Post #129: "Restructured as long 4 weeks total 160hrs is still ok, the hrs will play around one".
  https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/some-nurse-shifts-are-like-finish-work-at-930-pm-and-then-start-work-at-7-am-the-next-day.6477324/page-7
- This fits the contract: 40 hours x 4 weeks = 160 hours = 20 shifts of 8 hours, so 8 non-work days in 28. That arithmetic is mine.

## 4. "Off after night", night blocks, and the sleep day

**Nurses write the post-night day as a separate code, "SD" (sleep day), next to "DO" (day off).**

- HWZ post #49 gives a sample roster: "P, DO, A, P, N, N, SD, A". The same post names the "dreaded PAPA shifts".
  https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/some-nurse-shifts-are-like-finish-work-at-930-pm-and-then-start-work-at-7-am-the-next-day.6477324/page-3
  Reading: two nights, then a sleep day, then straight back to an AM shift. SD and DO are different codes. So in this ward the sleep day is not written as the off day.
- HWZ post #126 complains that managers "purposely put 2-3 night shift gao gao before your rest day". The nurse then says "go home sleep until tmr le". The rest day is lost to recovery sleep. This is the core grievance behind "off after night".
  https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/some-nurse-shifts-are-like-finish-work-at-930-pm-and-then-start-work-at-7-am-the-next-day.6477324/page-7
- Night block length: both the roster sample (N, N) and the complaint (2 to 3 nights) point to blocks of 2 to 3 nights on rotating rosters. Some nurses do permanent nights instead. The NTFGH clinician "took on permanent night shift duties about a year ago".
  https://nuhsplus.edu.sg/article/from-dusk-till-dawn--the-life-of-a-night-shift-nurse
- Unconfirmed: an official Singapore maximum for consecutive nights. I found none in MOH replies, the MOM pages or the HSEU material I was able to read.
- Unconfirmed: whether hospitals count SD toward the "8 off days" quota. The forum shows SD as its own code, but no source says how it is counted.

**Legal test (my derivation from the MOM rule above).** A night ends about 07:30. If the next shift is an AM at 07:00 the following day (the "N, SD, A" case), the gap is about 23.5 hours. That is less than the 30 continuous hours MOM allows for a shift worker's rest day. So a lone sleep day before an AM shift cannot be the statutory rest day where Part 4 applies. A sleep day plus one full day off gives about 47.5 hours, which is enough.

## 5. Quick returns and fatigue signals

- KTPH (NHG) says it has done "strategic roster reengineering that eliminates disruptive PAPA shift patterns (afternoon-morning-afternoon-morning rotations)". It says this "reduces fatigue".
  https://www.nhghealth.com.sg/ktph/for-health-professionals/nursing
- MP Louis Ng spoke in Parliament on 2022-09-12. The report says "a lot of nurses" told him they get four or five hours of rest between shifts. His words: "I fear not only for the nurses, but also for the level of care that patients receive from someone that is overworked."
  https://theindependent.sg/louis-ng-asks-moh-to-review-nurse-to-patient-ratio-says-theres-a-need-to-really-look-into-nurses-workload/
- The HWZ thread starter (2021): "If finish work at 930 pm, no 7 am shift the next day." Post #58: "Afternoon shift followed by morning shift" is "very common for healthcare staff".
  https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/some-nurse-shifts-are-like-finish-work-at-930-pm-and-then-start-work-at-7-am-the-next-day.6477324/
  https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/some-nurse-shifts-are-like-finish-work-at-930-pm-and-then-start-work-at-7-am-the-next-day.6477324/page-3
- Singapore study (Teo et al., Annals Academy of Medicine Singapore, 2023, 634 staff at 4 tertiary hospitals): "depression was predicted by sustained night shift work (2.15 [1.06–4.39])". Also "burnout was predicted by recently increased and sustained levels of longer working hours".
  https://annals.edu.sg/healthcare-worker-job-burnout-anxiety-and-depression-a-one-year-comparison-during-covid-19-in-singapore/
- Unconfirmed: a Singapore study that links post-night rest to incidents or errors. I did not find one. Searches of PubMed-indexed and Proceedings of Singapore Healthcare terms returned only non-Singapore studies.

## 6. Requests, flexibility and fairness in manual rosters

- SingHealth (ST, 2024-05-18): nurses "work on a monthly roster cycle" and "can request the specific shifts they prefer as long as they can meet their weekly working hours".
  https://www.sgh.com.sg/news/careers/hospitals-in-singapore-make-flexible-shifts-more-accessible-to-nurses
- TTSH focus groups found a "Lack of flexibility in shift start and end times". TTSH is building "artificial intelligence-supported software which allows the generation of full shifts and rosters".
  https://www.ttsh.com.sg/About-TTSH/TTSH-News/Pages/Tan-Tock-Seng-Hospital-Strives-To-Make-Work-Life-Balance-A-Reality-For-Nurses-With-Unique-Flexible-Shift-Scheduling.aspx
- IHH Singapore (private, GovInsider on CHI Innovate 2025): "Traditional rostering was tedious". With NurseShift.ai, "Up to 87 per cent of the shift requests are now being fulfilled".
  https://govinsider.asia/intl-en/article/pushing-ai-to-the-front-of-house-in-healthcare
- Requests cost other nurses. HWZ post #130: long work streaks happen "when staffing not good due to alot of nurses shift request". On extra shifts: "they can ask but u can always reject".
  https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/some-nurse-shifts-are-like-finish-work-at-930-pm-and-then-start-work-at-7-am-the-next-day.6477324/page-7
- Unconfirmed: a published cap on requests per nurse per month, a self-rostering scheme in a Singapore ward, and any weekend or public-holiday fairness rule. I looked in the ST/SGH, TTSH, KTPH, MOH and HSEU material above and found none.

## Implications for the roster rules

Labels: HARD = legal or safety must. STRONG = safety preference, high penalty, breakable only with a logged reason.

| # | Rule | Label | Evidence | Sources |
|---|------|-------|----------|---------|
| R1 | At least 1 full rest day in every 7 days. Never more than 6 work days in a row. | HARD | strong | HSEU via Mothership ("one rest day per week"), MOM (12-day max gap), MOH 2022 |
| R2 | The calendar day after the last night of a block has no shift at all (sleep day). | HARD | moderate | HWZ #49 "N, N, SD", HWZ #126, KTPH fatigue rationale, Teo 2023, user safety stance |
| R3 | The sleep day does NOT count as an off day or as the weekly rest day. | HARD for the weekly rest day, STRONG for the off quota | moderate | HWZ #49 (SD separate from DO), MOM 30-hour shift-worker rest day, my 23.5-hour derivation |
| R4 | After a night block: sleep day plus at least 1 full off day before the next shift (N...N, SD, DO). This gives about 47.5 hours and meets the 30-hour rule. | STRONG | moderate | MOM 30-hour rule, HWZ #126 complaint that the rest day is lost to sleep |
| R5 | Max 3 consecutive nights. Default target 2 to 3. | STRONG | weak | HWZ #49 and #126 only. No Singapore rule found |
| R6 | No PM followed by AM next day (quick return of about 9 to 10 hours). Also bans PAPA. | STRONG | moderate | KTPH removes PAPA to reduce fatigue, Louis Ng 2022, HWZ thread starter |
| R7 | No shift directly after a night except the sleep day (no N then AM or PM). | HARD | strong | Follows from shift times: N ends 07:30 to 08:00, so N then AM overlaps and N then PM leaves about 5 hours |
| R8 | Off-day quota: 8 non-work days per 28-day period for a 40-hour rotating nurse. Sleep days are extra. | STRONG | moderate | HSEU 40 h/week averaged, HWZ #129/#130 "8 off days", 160 h per 4 weeks |
| R9 | Max 12 hours per shift. | HARD | strong | MOM 12-hour daily cap, TTSH night is 12 h |
| R10 | Weekly hours stay at or under the contract cap over 2 to 3 weeks. Caps: 40 h rotating, 42 h regular, 38 to 40 h permanent night. Excess is overtime. Flag it. Do not ban it. | STRONG | strong | Mothership on HSEU agreements, MOH Jan 2022 |
| R11 | Shift requests are soft. Balance granted requests, nights and weekends across nurses over time. | soft (preference) | weak | SingHealth, TTSH, IHH. No Singapore request cap or weekend rule found |

Notes for the solver: R2 and R7 overlap. R2 is the rule the user cares about. R3 matters most for counting: if SD counts as an off day, the solver will "pay" the off quota with sleep and nurses lose real rest. That is exactly the HWZ #126 complaint.
