What changes in all three
  • Ink ramp softened — no pure/near black anywhere; headings 78% ink, body 55%.
  • Corners: cards 14–22px, chips and buttons pill or 9–12px.
  • Borders replaced by tinted surfaces and one soft shadow; grid hairlines only inside tables.
  • Shift chips carry colour meaning (AM / PM / Night / rest) instead of relying on mono type.
Things to decide with you
  • Keep dark mode? Warm palettes need a separate warm-dark ramp, not an inversion.
  • Night shift colour: cool blue/violet in all three, to read as "after hours" at a glance.
  • Photography or illustration in the empty states — I'd use placeholders until you supply ward photos.
  • Density: warmer shapes cost vertical space; the 28-day grid may need a compact toggle.
